Batching vs. Queueing

  • Batching: Groups events by a criteria (e.g., "when we hit 50 items" or "every 5 seconds"). The goal is efficiency—reducing overhead by making one big trip instead of many small ones.
  • Queueing: Handles events sequentially, usually because the system is resource-constrained. The goal is sustainability—making sure the system doesn't crash under load, even if processing items individually takes longer.

📦 The Shipping Analogy

Batching is like a delivery truck that only leaves the warehouse once it's full. Queueing is like a single-lane bridge where traffic must cross one by one so the structure doesn't collapse.
