Execution Timing Patterns

Mastering how to control function execution, manage resource consumption, and build responsive applications.

🏢

Debouncing

Waiting for a period of silence before executing, perfect for search bars and auto-saves.
☕

Throttling

Ensuring a function runs at a consistent, steady rate regardless of how fast it's triggered.
🛑

Rate Limiting

Restricting operations to a strict quota to protect against abuse and system overload.
🛒

Queueing

Buffering events to ensure they are all processed sequentially without crashing the system.
📦

Batching

Grouping events to process them as one unit, increasing efficiency by reducing overhead.
